What it computes

  • Total budget: (1 − SLO) × 30 days, in minutes. At 99.9%, that's 43.2 minutes a month. That's all of them.
  • Burn rate: downtime consumed ÷ budget elapsed so far. 1.0 = on the line. 3.0 = breach in ~10 days at this pace.
  • Breach projection: straight-line extrapolation of your current burn against the remaining budget.
  • The verdict line: a plain-language deploy call: green (ship), amber (change-freeze on risky deploys only), red (freeze + incident review).
